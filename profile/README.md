We believe using a computer will become less about reaching for a device.
We'll speak when words are enough, and look when something needs to be seen.
Conversation is powerful, but seeing gives us another way to understand,
imagine and decide. The interface should adapt to what each moment needs.

### Where we're starting

**Agents, built in conversation.** On Duvi, you describe an agent in plain
language, give it your business knowledge, and deploy it on your website,
WhatsApp and phone. It answers questions, qualifies leads, helps with
purchases and resolves support requests, grounded in what your business
actually knows.

### Open source

This is where we build in the open:

- **[agent-prompts](https://github.com/duvi-ai/agent-prompts):** a tested system
  prompt for AI agents on chat, voice, WhatsApp and phone, with what we
  measured writing it.
- **[skills](https://github.com/duvi-ai/skills):** engineering skills for coding
  agents, from building Duvi with them every day.
- **Duvi packages:** SDKs, components and tools for bringing Duvi agents
  into your own products and workflows.
